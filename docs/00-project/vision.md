# Product vision

Status: Proposed
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-10-07

**RiskLens: AI-powered Credit Risk Intelligence Platform**.
RiskLens serves credit risk analysts and Data teams that need to inspect loan portfolios, detect data defects, and explain findings against internal policies. The problem is fragmented calculations, evidence, and interpretation. The proposed product links all three within a traceable analysis run.

An analyst imports synthetic snapshots, inspects quality and metrics, investigates signals, retrieves policy evidence, and reviews recommendations. AI provides interpretation and evidence access. Product value must not depend on an unrestricted conversation or a collection of agents.

The v1 target is a demonstrable, auditable local application. It is not a regulatory certification or loan origination system. Success is measured by acceptance and release criteria. Python, SQL, data engineering, and AI are demonstrated through justified transformations, queryable persistence, contracts, and evaluations.
